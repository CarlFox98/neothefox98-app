Privacy policy

NeoTheFox98.exe · last updated November 2, 2026

Who uses it

NeoTheFox98.exe is a personal tool. Its only user is NeoTheFox98, who runs it on his own computer to manage his own Twitch and YouTube channels.

What it accesses

  • Twitch: NeoTheFox98's account, channel information, followers, subscriptions and stream events, through the Twitch API.
  • YouTube: NeoTheFox98's channel, videos and analytics, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on NeoTheFox98's own computer. Sign-in tokens and data are stored locally and are never sold, shared with or sent to any third party, except the Twitch and Google APIs themselves when the tool makes a request.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from YouTube is used only to show NeoTheFox98 his own channel information and to manage his own channel.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the sign-out button in the tool, from Google account permissions, or from Twitch connections. Removing access stops all use of the data, and local data can be deleted by removing the tool.
